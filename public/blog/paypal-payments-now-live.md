# New Payment Method: Pay with PayPal on Druxio

*March 19, 2026 · 4 min read · Announcement*
*By The Druxio Team · Product*

We've integrated PayPal as our primary payment platform, bringing secure escrow-protected transactions, debit and credit card support, and global buyer protection to every order on Druxio.

---

We're excited to announce that **PayPal is now the primary payment method on Druxio**. Starting today, every transaction on the platform is powered by PayPal's secure infrastructure -- bringing world-class buyer protection, global card acceptance, and faster payouts to both clients and experts.

## Why We Made the Switch

Since launch, our top priority has been making payments feel safe, fast, and transparent. After careful evaluation, we chose PayPal's Commerce Platform as our payment backbone for several key reasons:

- **Global reach** -- PayPal supports 200+ markets and 25+ currencies, making Druxio accessible to experts and clients worldwide
- **Built-in buyer protection** -- every transaction is covered by PayPal's Purchase Protection programme
- **Familiar checkout experience** -- millions of people already have a PayPal account, reducing friction at checkout
- **Card support without a PayPal account** -- clients can pay with Visa, Mastercard, or Amex directly through the checkout flow, no PayPal registration required

## How Payments Work Now

The flow is streamlined and secure:

**For Clients:**

- Accept a quote from an expert on your task
- You're taken to a dedicated checkout page showing a full order summary, price breakdown, and trust signals
- Choose to pay with your PayPal balance or directly with a debit or credit card
- Your payment is held in escrow until you confirm delivery -- you're always protected

**For Experts:**

- Complete the task and mark it as delivered
- Once the client confirms (or after the 3-day auto-release window), funds are released to your account
- Withdraw your earnings instantly via PayPal Payout to your email address
- A transparent 5% platform fee is applied at payout -- no hidden charges

## The Checkout Experience

We've built a dedicated, full-page checkout that feels professional and trustworthy. When you accept an expert's quote, you'll see:

- **Order summary** with task details, expert profile, and rating
- **Clear price breakdown** showing the service price, platform fee, and total
- **Escrow protection notice** confirming your funds are safe until delivery
- **PayPal and card payment buttons** integrated directly into the page
- **Trust signals** including SSL encryption status and buyer protection badges

No popups, no redirects to external sites. The entire experience stays within Druxio.

## Escrow Protection: Your Safety Net

Every payment on Druxio goes through escrow. Here's what that means in practice:

- When you pay, your money is held securely -- not sent directly to the expert
- The expert completes the work and marks it as delivered
- You review the delivery and confirm you're satisfied
- Only then are funds released to the expert

If something goes wrong, you can raise a dispute within a 24-hour negotiation period. Our admin team mediates to ensure a fair outcome. Experts can also voluntarily refund at any time, which instantly credits your balance.

If you don't confirm or dispute within 3 days of delivery, funds are automatically released to the expert with a 5-star rating -- so the system stays fair for everyone.

## Expert Payouts

Experts can withdraw their earnings at any time from the Wallet page. Payouts are processed as instant PayPal transfers to the email address on file. No minimum threshold, no waiting period once funds are released.

We believe experts should get paid quickly and without hassle. That's exactly what this integration delivers.

## What About Security?

All payment data is handled entirely by PayPal's PCI-compliant infrastructure. Druxio never sees, stores, or processes card numbers. Every transaction is encrypted with 256-bit SSL, and PayPal's fraud detection systems monitor for suspicious activity around the clock.
